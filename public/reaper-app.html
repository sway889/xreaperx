Built for three roles, one platform

Owners approve access, mentors brand their EA, clients trade with it.

Mentor-owned EAs

Every mentor names their EA, uploads an image and defines its symbol list — clients see it the moment they log in.

Unique license keys

Each client gets a key tied to their mentor's EA. Once activated on a device, it can never be reused.

AI chart scanner

Clients scan a symbol on demand — the platform surfaces a direction, entry, stop loss and take profit before anything is sent to the account.

MT4 / MT5 connect

Clients link their own trading account and their own MetaAPI token, so usage scales with what they've paid for.

Owner-verified access

Every mentor and client is reviewed and approved by the platform owner before they can log in.

Mentor dashboard

Mentors track every client, key and EA they run from a dashboard built only for them.

From sign-up to a running EA

Three steps to a connected, license-protected account.

1

Sign up & get approved

Register as a client with your mentor's ID, or as a mentor. The owner reviews access requests.

2

Enter your license key

First login only — enter the key your mentor issued. It's remembered from then on.

3

Connect & scan

Add your MT4/MT5 login and MetaAPI token, then run the AI scanner and start your EA.
